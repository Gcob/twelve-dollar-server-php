## 🟠 MAJOR (should be fixed)

### 2. The fold can never say "unclassified" for the server: a renamed step function moves its cost to another step without a sound

`lab/profile/fold-lib.php:79` · **Certainty:** verified

- **Why:** a sample takes the step of the innermost frame that a rule names, and `DiyServer::run` (rule: loop) is on
  every stack of the server: all 57,666 samples of the committed `profile.collapsed` go through it. An unnamed frame
  inherits the step of the nearest named caller. `lab/README.md:823` promises the opposite ("a rename in the app shows
  up there, not as a wrong step"), and `check_profile_json`'s `unclassified_share < 0.05` can't fail. The baseline
  already shows it on a small scale: `DiyProfile::write` and its closure (20 samples, the profiler's own flush) are
  unnamed, counted in "event loop", and the table says unclassified 0.0 %.
- **Scenario:** the step lab renames `feed_query` to `feed_fetch`, or adds a `feed_cache_get` under `route_feed`, and
  forgets `steps.rules`. Their samples land on `route_feed` (S3): the fold shows S3 route at about 30 % of `GET /feed`,
  unclassified 0 %, the check passes, and the ranking points at the router. Same for a renamed
  `DiyConnection::advance`: the `fwrite` cost moves from S8 to S2.
- **Flips if:** "a helper inherits its caller's step" is the intended design. Then the README sentence is wrong, and
  the rename case still needs a guard.
- **Fix:** classify by the leaf frame (the calibration shows internal functions never appear, so the leaf is always a
  PHP function): an unnamed leaf is unclassified, and the rules name the remaining ones (`app_count`,
  `DiyProfile::write`, the `{closure:` frames). Or keep the inheritance, list the absorbed leaf frames under the table,
  and count them in the threshold. Test: make the fixture's `like_insert;mysteryFrame` case come out unclassified or
  listed.

### 3. The baseline's p99 and its stalls may come partly from the per-second probes, and `realistic.sh` can't run without them

`lab/realistic.sh:414`, `lab/realistic.sh:418`, `lab/results/2026-11-22-realistic-baseline/README.md:411` ·
**Certainty:** plausible

- **Why:** every realistic run starts the sampler, which reads the server's `smaps_rollup` (a walk of its page tables
  under its memory-map lock) and lists its 64,500 open fds every second, and the poll reads `smaps_rollup` again each
  second (`probe poll` → `memory-now`). The 11 ms longest callback in every second (I get medians of 11.26 to 11.38 ms
  over the four holds) shows up only with 60,000 idle connections. The Q10 `posts.js` runs, with about 700 fds, stay
  at 0.5 ms. The README already names the sampler as the first suspect and leaves "a run without the sampler" to the
  next phase (`:439`), but no switch exists for it.
- **Scenario:** the step lab compares S1-b (several processes with `SO_REUSEPORT`) against the baseline's p99 of
  28.29 ms. The sampler loops over `cgroup.procs`, so it now walks N `smaps_rollup` files a second: the probes'
  interference changes with the variant, and the p99 difference goes to `SO_REUSEPORT`.
- **Flips if:** one hold with the sampler and the poll's `memory-now` off shows the same 11 ms longest callback and
  the same 16 to 21 stalled seconds.
- **Fix:** add a `--no-series` switch (no sampler, a poll that keeps only k6's memory and the aggregator's status),
  run one hold with it, and until then label the p99 and the stall counts as "with the probes on".
