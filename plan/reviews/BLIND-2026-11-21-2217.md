## 🟠 MAJOR (should be fixed)

### 1. The second finalist was chosen on a 1.5 % gap that the README itself calls noise

`README.md:132-134` (the finalists), `:379-383` (prediction 5), `:446-447` ("only 3 % behind", "0.8 µs/req more") ·
**Certainty:** verified (from the README's own tables)

- **Why:** `README.md:95-97` sets the rule: "differences under about 5 % are not distinguishable from the spread of the 3
  repeats". On posts, Swoole is at 32.83 (31.62 to 33.58) and Workerman nocache at 33.32 (31.5 to 34.36): 1.5 %, with
  overlapping ranges, and Workerman's range contains Swoole's median. On `/noop` it is 3.0 %, also overlapping. Yet the
  finalists line, prediction 5 ("finalists Swoole and DIY: held") and "Surprising" read the order as a result. The only
  noise caveat is the one for DIY against Swoole (`:465`).
- **Scenario:** a reader takes "Swoole second, Workerman third" as measured and accepts that series B (9,500 req/s)
  dropped Workerman. With another 3 repeats, Workerman's 31.5 run alone could swap the two. The runtime carried
  forward would then rest on a coin toss that nobody flagged.
- **Flips if:** the plan treats the finalist rule as mechanical on purpose, and nothing downstream depends on
  Workerman's rank. Then it is a 🟡 wording fix.
- **Fix:** say in the finalists section and in prediction 5 that the second place is a tie within the spread (posts
  1.5 %, ranges overlapping), and write "within the noise" instead of "3 % behind" and "0.8 µs/req more". If the choice
  matters later, run Workerman nocache through series B as well (6 runs).
